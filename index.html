What I do

Full-stack
marketing.

01 Social Media Management Instagram · TikTok · LinkedIn

Running accounts is easy. Building a system that compounds is not.

I manage Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn as interconnected channels — not separate feeds. Each platform gets its own format logic, cadence and tone, but all serve one brand narrative. I track what performs, document what works, and iterate every month.

Includes
  • Monthly content calendar & posting cadence
  • Caption writing in EN / RU / ES
  • Community management
  • Monthly analytics report
  • Format testing & iteration
02 Content Strategy Editorial systems · Masterplans

A strategy that sits in a folder is not a strategy.

Before I write a single brief, I spend time inside the competitive landscape — mapping what every rival already owns emotionally and visually, and what gap the brand can step into. The output is a system: formats, rhythms, pillars, and a 12-month roadmap that a team can actually use.

Includes
  • Competitor analysis (10–15 brands)
  • Audience portrait development
  • Brand positioning & voice document
  • Content matrix with pillars & formats
  • 12-month strategic masterplan
  • Monthly content plans — every post mapped out
  • Story briefs & post templates
  • Seasonal & cultural calendar
04 Brand Operations Reporting · Workflows · Ops

Good marketing breaks down without someone running the backend.

I handle the operational layer that keeps content moving — workflows, reporting, vendor coordination, and the systems that make a small team feel larger than it is.

Includes
  • Content workflow design
  • Editorial calendar management
  • Vendor & creator coordination
  • KPI dashboard setup
  • Weekly & monthly reporting
  • Process documentation
05 Localization RU · EN · IT · ES · BY

Translation gets the words right. Localization gets the meaning right.

I work natively in Russian, English, Spanish, Italian and Belarusian. I don't translate posts — I rewrite them for the platform, the audience, and the cultural context. A caption that works in Moscow reads differently than one that works in Madrid or Milan.

Includes
  • Copy adaptation RU / EN / ES / IT / BY
  • Platform-native rewriting — not literal translation
  • Cultural tone calibration per market
  • Caption & hook reformatting per language
  • Hashtag & keyword localisation
Selected work

Case studies.

Hair Care · HSB / Thiocyn GmbH · Hamburg, 2026
Full Content System for a Science-Backed Hair Care Brand. DACH Market.
The brief
Thiocyn had a clinically proven product and 30 years of research — but no system for communicating it without sounding like every other fear-driven hair loss brand on the market.
What was built
Competitor Intelligence Report — 8 brands (Alpecin, Plantur, Regaine, Hims, Nutrafol and others), ad library analysis, psychological trigger mapping. Brand Manifesto with communication rules — what we say, what we never say. 2026 Masterplan across four quarterly phases. Weekly content rhythm: 4 pillars, 5 formats. Evergreen Formats Library. Highlights Architecture. HWG-compliance guidelines for DACH. Monthly content calendars with every post mapped out. Story briefs, caption frameworks, hook library. Posts, carousels, reels — first full production cycle.
Outcome
Found the white space between Alpecin's aggression and Hims' lifestyle aesthetics. Clinical credibility without fear. The brand speaks like a doctor who also happens to care.
Reference
"The assigned tasks were completed consistently to our full satisfaction."
— Peter Hart, CEO · House of Sustainable Brands / Thiocyn GmbH
Sunglasses · Leipzig, Germany · 43,000+ customers · 2026
Brand Narrative & Content System for a Lifestyle Sunglasses Label.
The brief
A German sunglasses brand built on wood, stone and bamboo — with a genuine founding story and 43,000+ customers — but no coherent content system and no articulated identity beyond "we make cool glasses."
What was built
Competitor analysis across 10+ lifestyle and sustainable accessory brands. Brand Manifesto with origin story — turning the city itself into a brand asset. Tone of Voice guide with before/after language rewrites. Content Strategy: 5 pillars, weekly rhythm (3 feed posts + 1 Reel + Stories). Visual Identity guidelines. Full hashtag architecture. KPI framework. Target audience portraits. 100 hooks for reels and captions. Monthly content calendar. Posts, carousels, reels — first production cycle.
Outcome
Repositioned from "sustainable accessory brand" to cultural brand with a city identity. The founding city stopped being a location and became a story no competitor could copy.
Slow Fashion · Europe · 2026
World-Building Content System for a Slow Fashion Brand.
The brief
A slow fashion brand with strong product and clear values — but content that looked like every other "nature + softness" lifestyle account. No distinct world, no reason to follow.
What was built
Competitor analysis of 10+ slow fashion and lifestyle brands. Audience portrait development. Seasonal brand narrative — nature as a living character, not a backdrop. Brand positioning and communication rules. Content matrix: 5 pillars, formats, cadence — each choice justified. 12-month strategic masterplan. Monthly production plan with every post mapped out. Story briefs and post templates. Posts, carousels, reels — first full content cycle.
Outcome
Moved the brand from "aesthetic clothing account" to emotional world — warmth, memory, protection. Nature became a story, not just a visual filter.
